# ADR-0016: Collect secretary digests from primary sources

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-27

In the context of replacing X-based discovery with useful unattended research
and technology briefings, facing paid or noisy social feeds, cron sessions that
cannot rely on conversational memory, and the risk of turning a bibliography
into another unreviewed inbox, we decided for separate paper and toolchain
Skills backed by primary bibliographic and upstream sources, profile-local
cutoff and disposition state, and user-selected Zotero writes, and against X or
social sources, an undifferentiated news digest, cron memory, or automatic
Zotero ingestion, to obtain reproducible and actionable summaries with explicit
coverage, accepting maintenance of source watchlists and local state.
